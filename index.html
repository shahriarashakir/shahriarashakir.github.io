Legal Interests & Commentary

Reading and writing on public law

Alongside my placement I follow developments in public, administrative and immigration law. Below is a short note, in my own words, on a reported and publicly available Court of Appeal decision that interests me.

Public Law · Article 8

IA & Ors v SSHD [2025] EWCA Civ 1516

Court of Appeal (Civil Division), 26 November 2025

This appeal arose from a Palestinian family of six in Gaza who applied for entry clearance outside the Immigration Rules to join a relative settled in the UK. Their applications were refused and their appeals dismissed by the First-tier Tribunal. The Court of Appeal clarified the test for establishing "family life" between adult relatives under Article 8 ECHR, reaffirming the principle in Kugathas v SSHD [2003] EWCA Civ 31 that more than normal emotional ties — real, committed or effective support, or additional elements of dependence — must be shown. It also considered when the State owes obligations to people outside its territory, and stressed that considerable weight must be given to the Home Secretary's immigration policy when assessing proportionality. The Secretary of State's appeal succeeded. I find the case interesting because it sits at the pressure point between individual human-rights protection and the executive's control of immigration policy, and shows how the appellate courts calibrate that balance.
